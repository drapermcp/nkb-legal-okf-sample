# § 6-3-603 - Crimes and OffensesChapter 3 - Offenses Against PropertyArticle 6 - FraudSection 6-3-603 - Possession of Forged Writings and Forgery Devices; Penalties

## Statutory Text

> (a) A person is guilty of a felony punishable by imprisonment for not more than five (5) years, a fine of not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000.00), or both, if he:
>
> (i) Possesses a writing knowing it is forged in a manner specified in W.S. 6-3-602(a)(i) or (ii) and intending to utter or pass it to defraud another person;
