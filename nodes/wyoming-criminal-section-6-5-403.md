# § 6-5-403 - Crimes and OffensesChapter 5 - Offenses Against Public AdministrationArticle 4 - Interference With Public ContractingSection 6-5-403 - Bid Rotating; Penalties; Prohibitions

## Statutory Text

> (a) A person commits bid rotating when, pursuant to any collusive scheme, plan or agreement with another, he engages in a pattern of submitting sealed bids to governmental entities with the intent that the award of those bids rotates or is distributed among persons that submit bids on a substantial number of the same or similar public contracts. For purposes of this subsection, a pattern of submitting sealed bids shall include not less than three (3) contract bids within a period of ten (10) years.
>
> (b) Bid rotating is a felony punishable by imprisonment for not more than five (5) years, a fine of not more than ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00), or both.
