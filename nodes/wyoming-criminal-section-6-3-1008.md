# § 6-3-1008 - Crimes and OffensesChapter 3 - Offenses Against PropertyArticle 10 - Animal AbuseSection 6-3-1008 - Use of Agricultural and Livestock Management Practices, Wildlife Management and Humane Destruction Not Prohibited

## Statutory Text

> (a) Nothing in this article may be construed to prohibit:
> 
> (i) A person humanely destroying an animal, including livestock;
> (v) The use of dogs or raptors in hunting;
