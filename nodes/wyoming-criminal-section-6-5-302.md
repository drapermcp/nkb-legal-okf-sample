# § 6-5-302 - Crimes and OffensesChapter 5 - Offenses Against Public AdministrationArticle 3 - Perjury and Criminal FalsificationSection 6-5-302 - Proof of Solicitation of Perjury

## Statutory Text

> In a trial for soliciting perjury, no conviction shall be had on the evidence of the person solicited, unsupported by other testimony.
