# § 6-6-301 - Crimes and OffensesChapter 6 - Offenses Against Public PeaceArticle 3 - Unlawful Conduct Within Governmental FacilitiesSection 6-6-301 - Definitions

## Statutory Text

> (a) As used in W.S. 6-6-301 through 6-6-307:
>
> (i) "Governing body" means any elected or appointed commission, board, agency, council, trustees or other body created or authorized by the laws of this state and vested with authority to perform specified governmental, educational, proprietary or regulatory functions;
